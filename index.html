About the solution

Each tree carries a tag. Its record holds species, position, a history of girth and height readings, and health. From the latest reading the portal estimates biomass and carbon. The same records draw the satellite view, fill the registry and can be edited from data entry.

Biomass and carbon per tree

Girth and height give above-ground biomass using a general tropical equation. Carbon is 47% of it, and CO₂ equivalent is carbon × 44/12. Try a tag.

Hover to inspect

Move the pointer over a tree on the map to see its tag, species, size, health, biomass and carbon. Click it to open the full record.

Open data, agency control

Satellite layers come from open archives. Records stay with the agency. See resources.
The equation is a placeholder. A pilot would replace it with a model calibrated on local field plots, and report uncertainty.
